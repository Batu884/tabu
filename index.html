FAQ

Common questions.

What does Tabu do?

Tabu designs, develops, hosts and maintains business websites, with SEO, AEO and GEO services for traditional search, answer engine discovery and generative AI visibility.

How much does a Tabu website cost?

Starter costs ₹18,000 and includes up to five pages, mobile-first design, a contact form, hosting, SSL and basic on-page SEO. Business costs ₹25,000 plus a retainer and adds local SEO, Google Maps setup, AEO and GEO integration, and monthly citation management. Custom projects are quoted around your requirements.

Does Tabu include search engine optimization?

Starter includes basic on-page SEO. Business adds local SEO, Google Maps setup, AEO and GEO integration with llms.txt and FAQ schema, plus monthly review and citation management. Search rankings and inclusion in AI-generated answers cannot be guaranteed.

How long does a website take?

It depends on size and scope. A small business site is typically a matter of weeks, not months. We'll give you a clear timeline once we understand what you need.

Do I need to sort out hosting myself?

No. We host your website on infrastructure we manage, set up your domain and SSL, and keep an eye on it for you.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. We can redesign it, rebuild it, move it onto our hosting, or simply take over maintenance of what you have.

Can I make changes to my site after launch?

Absolutely. On a maintenance plan, just send us what you'd like changed and we'll handle it.

Will my website work on phones?

Every site we build is designed mobile-first and tested across phones, tablets and desktops.
